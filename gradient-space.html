Our Space

Gradient Space

A space for running experiments with users and robots, testing and developing our methods, holding workshops, and coming together.

About

Where physical meets digital

What makes the Gradient Space special is its equipment. It lets us capture the real world, connect physical and digital worlds, and create new experiences. It is where our methods move from development to testing with real people, real robots, and real environments.
